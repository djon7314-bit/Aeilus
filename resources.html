Free Tools

Resources

Everything here is free. No strings, no signups, no catch. Just tools we believe in across every area of your life. Organized by pillar so you can find what you actually need.

If You're Struggling Right Now

You don't have to be in crisis to reach out. But if you are — these are here for you, any time of day or night.

  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline — Call or text 988, available 24/7
  • Crisis Text Line — Text HOME to 741741
  • SAMHSA National Helpline — 1-800-662-4357, free and confidential, 24/7
  • International Crisis Centres — iasp.info — find support anywhere in the world

The Reset Room

A free interactive wellness tool built for the moments when you need to slow down, breathe, and reset. No account needed. No strings attached.

  • Box Breathing — a guided breathing exercise to calm your nervous system
  • Grounding Tool — bring yourself back to the present moment
  • The Unsent Letter — say what you need to say, without sending it
  • Quiet Moment Timer — just stillness, for however long you need
Visit The Reset Room

Mind

The inner work. Where everything starts.

  • Psychology Today Therapist Finder — Search therapists by location, specialty, and insurance
  • Open Path Collective — Affordable therapy finder for those who need it
  • Insight Timer — Free meditation and mindfulness app with thousands of guided sessions
  • Woebot — Free AI-powered mental health support tool

Body

Move because it keeps you alive and sharp.

  • Darebee — Free workout plans for all levels, no equipment needed
  • MyFitnessPal — Free activity and movement tracker
  • Nike Training Club — Free guided workouts for all fitness levels
  • American Heart Association — Free health tools, calculators, and guidance for long-term cardiovascular health

Fuel

What you put in drives everything.

  • Cronometer — Free detailed nutrition tracker that goes beyond calories
  • Examine.com — Free evidence-based supplement and nutrition research
  • MyPlate — Free USDA nutrition guidance, straightforward and practical
  • Huberman Lab — Free podcast and resources on nutrition, brain health, and performance

Knowledge

Read more. Think better. Compound everything.

  • Libby — Free ebooks and audiobooks through your local library card
  • Project Gutenberg — Thousands of free classic books, no account needed
  • Blinkist — Book summaries for men who want the key ideas fast
  • Goodreads — Free reading tracker and book discovery platform

Financial

Money stress is real. These tools help you get your footing.

  • Mint — Free budgeting and expense tracking tool
  • Annual Credit Report — Check your credit report for free, once a year from each bureau
  • Consumer Financial Protection Bureau — Free financial education tools, guides, and resources
  • National Foundation for Credit Counseling — Free and low-cost financial counseling and debt guidance
  • Investopedia — Free financial literacy education, from budgeting basics to investing fundamentals

Connection

Start with yourself. Build outward from there.

  • 16Personalities — Free Myers-Briggs style personality assessment. A good starting point for understanding how you're wired.
  • Truity Enneagram Test — Free Enneagram assessment. Goes deeper into motivation and behavior patterns.
  • Meetup — Find local groups built around shared interests. A low-pressure way to meet people.
  • Aeilus Community — Our own community space for men doing this work. Coming soon.
