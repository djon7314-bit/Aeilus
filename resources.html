If You Need Help Right Now

Crisis & Emergency Support

These resources are free, confidential, and available around the clock. You don't have to be in a full-blown crisis to reach out — if something feels wrong, that's enough.

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

Free, confidential support for people in distress. Available 24/7 by call or text. Covers suicidal thoughts, mental health crises, and emotional distress of any kind.

Call or Text: 988

24/7 · Free · Confidential

Crisis Text Line

If talking feels like too much, text HOME to 741741 to connect with a trained crisis counselor. Free, confidential, available 24/7.

Text HOME to 741741

24/7 · Text-Based · Free

The Trevor Project

Crisis intervention and suicide prevention for LGBTQ+ young people under 25. Call, text, or chat — available 24/7.

1-866-488-7386 · Text START to 678-678

LGBTQ+ Youth · 24/7 · Free

SAMHSA National Helpline

Free, confidential treatment referrals and information for mental health and substance use disorders. Available in English and Spanish.

1-800-662-4357

24/7 · Free · English & Spanish

Low-Cost & Free Therapy

Therapy Without the Price Tag

Therapy is valuable — and it's often inaccessible. These options exist specifically for people who need support but face financial barriers.

Open Path Collective

Connects people with therapists who offer sessions at $30–$80 — significantly below standard rates. One-time membership fee of $65 for lifetime access.

openpathcollective.org

Reduced Cost · Licensed Therapists

Psychology Today Therapist Finder

Search for therapists in your area who offer sliding scale fees — meaning they adjust their rate based on what you can afford.

psychologytoday.com

Sliding Scale · Search Tool

NIMH Treatment Locator

The National Institute of Mental Health's guide to finding community mental health centers, which often offer free or income-based services.

nimh.nih.gov

Community Centers · Income-Based

NAMI HelpLine

Free peer support and resource referrals from the National Alliance on Mental Illness. Not a crisis line — more of a knowledgeable friend who helps you navigate the system.

1-800-950-6264

Peer Support · Resource Navigation

Apps & Digital Tools

Free Tools for Daily Support

These apps won't replace therapy, but they can meaningfully support your mental health between sessions — or when professional help isn't accessible yet.

Headspace

Guided meditation and mindfulness. Offers a free basic tier with foundational practices for stress, anxiety, and sleep.

headspace.com

Meditation · Free Tier Available

Insight Timer

One of the largest free meditation libraries available. Thousands of guided meditations, sleep content, and mindfulness courses at no cost.

insighttimer.com

Meditation · Mostly Free

Woebot

An AI-powered mental health chatbot grounded in CBT principles. Not a replacement for therapy, but a useful, judgment-free space to process thoughts.

woebothealth.com

CBT-Based · AI Support · Free

7 Cups

Free online chat with trained volunteer listeners. Not therapists, but real humans who are there to listen without judgment — available anytime.

7cups.com

Peer Listening · Free · Anonymous
