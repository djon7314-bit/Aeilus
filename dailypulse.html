Evening Ritual

Daily Pulse

Five pillars. Five questions. A few honest minutes at the end of the day. This isn't a performance — it's just you, checking in with yourself.

Pillar 01

Mind

"How present were you with yourself today?"

Scattered
Grounded

What was your mind carrying today? What do you want to leave here before tomorrow?

Pillar 02

Body

"How did you treat your body today?"

Neglected
Intentional

Did you move, rest, or listen to what your body needed? What did it tell you?

Pillar 03

Fuel

"What did you put into yourself today?"

Running on empty
Nourished

Food, water, what you consumed mentally — did it serve you? What would you change tomorrow?

Pillar 04

Knowledge

"Did you feed your mind something worthwhile today?"

Autopilot
Intentional

What did you read, learn, or think about? What's one thing that stayed with you?

Pillar 05

Connection

"How did you show up for yourself and others today?"

Isolated
Connected

Did you have a real conversation? Did you honor your own energy? What did connection feel like today?

"Take these reflections off the screen. Write them down."

Your future self will want to read this. A journal captures what a screen can't hold — the pattern of your days, the arc of your becoming. Any journal works. But if you want one built around this practice, the Aeilus Journal was made for exactly this.
