A Letter from the Founder

"I didn't start Aeilus to build an empire."

I started it because I know what it feels like to need something — a tool, a resource, a moment of clarity — and not have it. And I know I'm not alone in that.

Aeilus exists to help people heal. That's it. Everything we create — every journal, every guide, every piece of content — is built around one question: does this actually help someone?

But there's something else I want you to know.

Whatever this brand earns, I intend to give almost all of it away. Not as a marketing strategy. Not for the tax write-off. Because there are people out there right now who don't have food, or a stable home, or access to the mental health support that could change everything for them. A single parent holding it together with nothing left in the tank. Someone sleeping outside tonight who deserves better. People in crisis who just need someone — or something — to show up for them.

I don't need a billion-dollar lifestyle. I need to know that what I'm building means something.

So when you support Aeilus, you're not just investing in your own wellbeing. You're part of something bigger. The two things aren't separate — taking care of yourself and caring about others. That's actually the whole point.

Welcome. I'm glad you're here.

— Dominick

Founder, Aeilus
